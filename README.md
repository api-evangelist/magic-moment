Magic Moment is a Tokyo-based AI sales-automation company whose Magic Moment Playbook platform captures real business facts from deals, calls, email, and meetings and writes them back into Salesforce, HubSpot, and other CRMs as structured sales data. Products include Magic Moment Playbook (AI auto-recording SFA/CRM), Playbook Capture, MM Claw, the Revenue Platform performance-based demand service, and the Magic Moment X managed AI department. The company publishes a Playbook API for developers and partners (announced 2024-04-03, documented at developer.magicmoment.co.jp behind the product login) and serves five anonymous Swagger 2.0 contracts for its Playbook integration services on its production hosts — Salesforce, HubSpot, Office suite (Google Workspace / Microsoft 365), call integration (MiiTel, Zoom Phone), and the reporting/aggregation service. The company is ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified and publishes an llms.txt.

## How this profile is made
